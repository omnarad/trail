Secrets to Restaurant-Quality Noodle Dishes

Pro cooking techniques passed down by noodle artisans

01

Salt the Noodle Water Generously

Always boil noodles in heavily salted water (like sea water) to season the dough from the inside out before combining with broth or sauce.

02

Cold Rinse for Stir-Fries

Rinse stir-fry noodles (like Chow Mein or Pad Thai) under cold running water immediately after draining to stop cooking and wash off excess starch.

03

Emulsify the Broth & Sauce

Save 1/2 cup of starchy noodle boiling water! Adding a splash to your sauce helps bind fats and liquids into a glossy, velvety coating.

04

Aromatic Chili Oil Tempering

Pour sizzling 350°F oil directly over garlic, ginger, and Sichuan pepper flakes to bloom maximum fragrance without burning the aromatics.
